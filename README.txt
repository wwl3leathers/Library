ASIMOV'S THREE LAWS - STORED AS A JOKE
A playful reference to Isaac Asimov's I, Robot, not operating instructions or a replacement for the user-control rule above.
Brief quotation: "A robot may not injure a human being"
The three laws, paraphrased:
1. Protect humans from harm, including harm caused by failing to act.
2. Follow human orders unless doing so would violate the first law.
3. Preserve the robot's own existence when that does not conflict with either higher law.
These are paraphrases, not the complete verbatim passage.
Reference: https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/5681/i-robot-by-isaac-asimov/paperback/

ORGANIZATION
Touchstones, Favorited, and Fantasy/escape:
  Category -> Complete Series/Novel or Incomplete Series -> Narrator -> Author -> Series -> Book order -> Title and release-date field.
Other categories:
  Category -> Author -> Series -> Book order -> Title and release-date field.
Authors are sorted by surname where applicable.
Narrator names appear once per group. Ensemble recordings stay grouped together.
A narrator change may split a series between narrator groups.
Bundles and alternate recordings remain distinct source entries.

COMPLETION RULE
Complete Series/Novel requires BOTH a finished story and the complete set in the supplied library.
A self-contained standalone novel qualifies even if the author could revisit it.
Missing volumes, ongoing series, and every doubtful case belong in Incomplete Series.
Incomplete does not automatically mean actively being written: it can mean an incomplete owned set, uncertain status, hiatus, or an unfinished story.
Do not add separate missing-volume flags.
Completion placements are dated research judgments, not permanent guarantees.
